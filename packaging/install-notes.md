## How to install

Click the link for your computer. It downloads the one file you need:

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | **[Blendity-Setup-Windows.exe](https://github.com/EmilianoManaloIV/blendity/releases/latest/download/Blendity-Setup-Windows.exe)** |
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer) | **[Blendity-macos.dmg](https://github.com/EmilianoManaloIV/blendity/releases/latest/download/Blendity-macos.dmg)** |
| Linux (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, or similar) | **[Blendity-linux-x86_64.AppImage](https://github.com/EmilianoManaloIV/blendity/releases/latest/download/Blendity-linux-x86_64.AppImage)** |

(These always fetch the newest version. This release's own files are in **Assets** below. You don't need
"Source code": GitHub adds it to every release for developers.)

- **Windows:** open the downloaded file. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then
  **Run anyway** (Blendity isn't signed with a paid certificate yet). Click **Next** through the steps, keeping
  "Blendity with Blender's libraries (recommended)", then **Install** and **Finish**. Blendity is in the Start menu.
- **Mac:** open the downloaded file and drag **Blendity** onto **Applications**. Open Blendity from Applications. The
  first time, macOS says it can't check the app: click **Done**, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**,
  scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Blendity (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Blendity, then **Open**).
- **Linux:** right-click the downloaded file, choose **Properties**, turn on **Allow executing file as program**
  (or "Executable as Program"), then double-click it.

The full guide, with uninstalling and what to do if something goes wrong, is in the
[README](https://github.com/EmilianoManaloIV/blendity#download).
