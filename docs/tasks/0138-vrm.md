# 0138: VRM characters

- **Status:** done
- **Requirements:** R-6
- **Decisions:** ADR 0047
- **Model:** main session
- **PR:** (with tasks 0136-0137)

## Goal
A VRM avatar (VRoid Studio, UniVRM; 0.x and 1.0) opens as a character: its humanoid bones named for the rig tools,
its expressions as shape keys, its spring bones, its MToon look with an outline, facing +Z.

## Scope
- In: `.vrm` read by the glTF reader (a VRM is a GLB), the extension handled by `import_vrm.cpp`:
  - 0.x: `extensions.VRM` (humanoid, blendShapeMaster, secondaryAnimation, materialProperties, meta);
  - 1.0: `VRMC_vrm` (humanoid, expressions, meta), `VRMC_springBone`, `VRMC_materials_mtoon`, `KHR_materials_unlit`.
- In: humanoid bones renamed (`leftUpperLeg` -> `LeftUpLeg`, the thumb's joints by each version's names), before the
  skin takes the names for its vertex groups.
- In: expressions (0.x groups with weights in percent; 1.0 presets and customs) as a shape key per bound mesh: the
  weighted sum of the morph targets it binds, under the expression's name.
- In: spring chains from each bone group's (0.x) or spring's first joint's (1.0) settings: stiffness / 4, drag as
  damping, gravity power, hit radius.
- In: MToon as Toon: the shade colour as a ratio of the lit colour, toony as softness, shift as threshold; cutout and
  transparent modes; cull off as double-sided; unlit shaders; an Outline per mesh from the widest world-space outline
  its materials ask for (0.x widths in centimetres, 1.0 in metres; screen-space outlines 3 mm).
- In: a 0.x model (facing -Z in glTF) turned half a turn about Y; a 1.0 model already faces +Z.
- In: the meta's title, author and license in the import's notes.
- Out: shade textures, rim light, matcaps, first-person settings, look-at.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A synthetic VRM 0.x and 1.0 (written by the tests): the bones named `Hips`, `Spine`, `Head`, `LeftUpLeg`,
   `LeftLeg`, `LeftFoot`; the left leg on the character's left and the hair behind the head in both.
2. The face's expressions `a` / `aa` (the whole first morph) and `blink` (half the second) as shape keys; the face
   faces +Z.
3. The hair a spring chain with stiffness 0.5, damping 0.4, gravity 0.5, radius 0.03; the material Toon with a shadow
   ratio of 0.5 and a 5 mm outline; the meta in the notes.
4. Dropped into the editor: its `.mat` made, the outline on the face, the lesson's Try-it.
