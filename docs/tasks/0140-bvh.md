# 0140: BVH motion capture

- **Status:** done

- **Decisions:** ADR 0047
- **Model:** main session

## Goal
Motion capture in BVH (CMU, Mixamo's exports, Rokoko and most capture tools) opens as a skeleton with its clip.

## Scope
- In: an own reader for the HIERARCHY (ROOT, JOINT, End Site, OFFSET, CHANNELS) and MOTION (Frames, Frame Time,
  rows); bounded (4,096 joints, 200,000 frames); a row cut short ends the clip there.
- In: joints as bones at their offsets (identity rest: BVH has none), end sites as end bones (so the last bone has a
  length); X mirrored like FBX; a skeleton taller than 10 units scaled from centimetres.
- In: the motion as a clip at 1 / Frame Time: rotation channels composed in the file's order, Euler keys nearest the
  frame before; position channels where present (the root, usually).
- In: the editor saves the clip as any imported one and plays it on the root (task 0139's path).
- Out: retargeting onto another rig (said in the lesson).

## Acceptance criteria
1. A synthetic BVH in centimetres: the skeleton in metres, the clip at the file's rate; at its end the hips have moved
   1 m towards +Z and the spine has bent 90 degrees about X.
2. Damaged files refuse or import without harm.
3. Dropped into the editor: the clip playing on the skeleton; the lesson's Try-it.
