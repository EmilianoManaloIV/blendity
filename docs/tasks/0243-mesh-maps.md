# 0243: mesh maps

- **Status:** done (round 263)
- **Requirements:** R-07, R-14
- **Decisions:** ADR 0079
- **Model:** main session
- **PR:** #209

## Asked
The user (2026-10-27): Substance Painter's pillars - the layer stack with masks, mesh maps and generators, smart
materials, painting on the model. The mesh maps come first: everything else reads them.

## Goal
One command bakes the five mesh maps (world-space normal, position, curvature, ambient occlusion, thickness) of a mesh
into its UV layout off the main thread, writes them as images under the project's Assets and records them on the
object, so the layer stack and the generators of the next rounds have what they need.

## What changed
- **`bake_mesh_maps`** (`src/render/mesh_maps.cpp`, ADR 0079): the texture bake's texel rasterizer (the UV layout -
  the mesh's own when they lie in 0-1 without overlap, the default, so a textured object keeps its look; else, or on
  `layout`, generated by `lightmap_uvs` - the texel owners and barycentrics) copied for the new bake, `own_uvs_usable`
  shared from `texture_bake.cpp` (moved out of its anonymous namespace; the texture bake itself is unchanged); per
  covered texel the interpolated world normal (as 0-1 colour), the position in the mesh's bounds, the vertex curvature
  interpolated, ambient occlusion and thickness by cosine-weighted hemisphere rays about the face's own outside (the
  smooth normal leans past a facet's plane near a vertex: a sphere read 0.94 open with it) through a `PathTracer` built
  on the mesh alone, over a reach of the bounds' longest side *in the world* (the bounds' corners placed by the object's
  matrix; the position map keeps the object's bounds); the padding dilated as the texture bake pads.
  `MeshMapsSettings {size, samples, padding, keep_uvs}`, `MeshMapsResult` with the five images, the coverage and the
  layout; `write_mesh_maps` to `<base>_normal.png`, `_position.png`, `_curvature.png`, `_ao.png`, `_thickness.png`.
- **Thickness** is the mean distance an inward ray travels before it meets the mesh, over the reach (a miss counts the
  whole reach): a thin plate reads near 0, a solid sphere about two thirds (a chord 2R cos(theta) under the cosine
  weighting averages 4R / 3 of a diameter's reach). The share-of-hits definition the spec first had made every closed
  shape read 1.
- **Curvature** (`vertex_curvature` in `src/render/mesh_maps.cpp`): per vertex, the mean over its ring of each
  neighbour's drop below the tangent plane over its chord squared - on a sphere of radius R every chord's sagitta is
  e^2 / 2R, so each term is 1 / 2R whatever the ring's arrangement and the mesh's resolution (the review's point: a
  value over the edge length shrank with the resolution and a dense sculpt came out flat) - times a quarter of the
  bounds' longest side so the object's scale drops out, two smoothing passes over the ring (a second difference
  amplifies surface grain: a ten-thousandth of radius noise on a 96 x 64 sphere spread it 0.43-0.82 with one pass,
  0.56-0.69 with two), mapped 0.5 + k / 2 into 0-1 (clamped): a sphere of half the bounds reads 0.625, a sharp edge on
  a dense mesh saturates.
- **In the editor** (`src/editor/mesh_maps_ui.cpp`): `meshmaps [size] [samples] [keepuvs|layout]` on the active mesh
  object, `meshmaps cancel`, `meshmaps status` (the record and whether it is stale); Object > Bake Mesh Maps (512, 1024
  or 2048) beside Bake Textures; the bake on a worker with the mesh copied bare (`MeshMapsJob`, the `RemeshJob` shape:
  a job of its own rather than a generalized one, the two have nothing but the thread in common; the body under
  `JobSystem::run_background`, so its parallel rows have an arena of their own), the worker also writing the PNGs
  (five images at 2,048 are not the frame's work); the status line says so, Esc over the Scene view or `meshmaps
  cancel` stops it. Edit Mode and Weight Paint are left at the start. When it lands and the mesh is still the one it
  was baked from (uid, version and deform_version) and no mode holds it, one undo step: the mesh's own UVs are kept
  byte for byte when they were usable (the default: the re-review found the first version replacing a textured
  object's UVs, scrambling its textures), else the generated layout is written onto the mesh as its UVs (as Bake
  Textures writes its; the log says so), the
  files are under `Assets/Textures/MeshMaps/<name>` (a new name every time, as Bake Textures names) and the
  MeshRenderer's `mesh_maps` and `mesh_maps_key` are set; the Project window refreshes. A mesh changed meanwhile, a
  cancel, a failure, a mode entered meanwhile: the result is dropped and its files removed. Hidden faces are baked (the
  bare copy's hidden flags cleared: they are part of the surface the maps describe). Refused while Play runs, for an
  object whose modifiers add or remove geometry (the maps would not fit the base mesh's layout; a deform's evaluated
  mesh is what is baked), and for a mesh with no faces. The Learn tab and the manual.
- **The key** (`mesh_maps_key_text`): the positions, corner list, UVs, face sizes, smoothing (per-face flags, sharp
  edges, Shade Smooth and its angle: the normal map reads them) and the object's world matrix (the maps are in the
  world) of the bake's source hashed to 16 hex digits - a content key, not the session's version counter (which
  restarts on every open), so a reopened scene reads current until the mesh, its shading or its placement changes.
  `meshmaps status` compares it with the current source's. A bake that did not land - dropped, cancelled, failed, or the
  editor closed over it - has its files removed by the job's destructor.
- **Saved:** `mesh_maps` and `mesh_maps_key` on the MeshRenderer in the scene file; reflected only when the reflector is
  serializing, so files, undo and hashing see them and the Inspector does not (an Inspector int had rounded through a
  float and marked the object edited on every selection: the review's finding).

## Scope
- Out: a high-poly source (baking a sculpt onto a low-poly target), bent normals, a GPU bake (the path tracer's GPU
  gather could do AO later), UDIMs, the maps shown in the viewport (round 0244's layer stack previews them), cavity as
  a separate map (curvature covers it).

## Acceptance criteria
1. A 48 x 32 UV sphere at 256 with 16 samples: ambient occlusion above 0.95 at every covered texel (nothing occludes a
   sphere from outside; 1.000 measured); the mean thickness within 0.05 of two thirds (0.665); curvature in 0.6-0.65
   within 0.05 of one value over the sphere (0.624-0.635; a 96 x 64 sphere's mean within 0.03 of it: 0.623, the
   resolution dropping out); the world normal at a texel within 3 degrees of the normalized position of the point it
   covers (1.7); the 96 x 64 sphere with a ten-thousandth of radius noise keeps its mean within 0.03 and its spread
   under 0.3 (0.624, 0.557-0.690).
2. An L of two cubes (one on top of the other, half a unit over): on the lower cube's top face within a twentieth of
   the upper cube's wall (read back through the position and normal maps) the AO's mean is below 0.8 and its minimum
   below 0.7 (0.536 and 0.188), the faces that meet read 0 and the open faces above 0.95; a thin plate (a cube scaled
   to 0.02 thick) has a mean thickness below 0.12 (0.052: its narrow sides are legitimately thick across) while a unit
   cube's mean is above 0.4 (0.628; a texel beside an edge is legitimately thin toward that edge); the same cube placed
   at a hundredth of its size reads the same thickness within 0.05 and AO above 0.95 (the reach follows the object into
   the world).
3. The bake runs on a worker: the editor's frames step while it works (the slowest printed: 4-14 ms over the runs,
   after the cold frames), the result lands as one undo step that keeps the mesh's usable own UVs byte for byte (the
   sample sphere's) or writes the layout onto the mesh (one UV per corner, inside 0-1) and sets the record (undo clears
   it; the files stay); a change made meanwhile drops it with a log line and removes its
   files while the first bake's files survive; cancel adds no file; the five PNGs exist.
4. The texture bake is unchanged (nothing of it was refactored but `own_uvs_usable`'s namespace): its tests pass.
5. The record round-trips through the scene file and the loaded mesh's key equals the record's (current after a
   reopen); `meshmaps status` says "stale" after the mesh is changed, after Shade Smooth is toggled and after the
   object is turned, and current again after each undo. The Inspector shows neither field (the layer-stack round,
   which shows the maps, gets the marker).

## Risks and edge cases
- A mesh with no faces or no area: refused with a reason. A mesh whose own UVs overlap: the generated layout, as the
  texture bake does (the record says which).
- Rays from a texel start a little off the surface along the face's outside (the bounds' longest side times 1e-4) and
  the hemisphere is about that outside, not the smooth normal, so a facet does not occlude itself or its neighbour.
- Time: 256 x 256 x 16 samples x 2 maps on the sphere in its own UVs is 2,039,808 rays (63,744 texels: the sphere's
  own layout fills the square), 27-37 ms in the test's runs (a 1,024 bake with 16 samples is sixteen times that). The
  worker holds a PathTracer of its own built on the copied mesh.
- A deform modifier's parameter, a pose or a shape weight changes the evaluated mesh without touching the base mesh's
  counters: the landing check reads the base mesh's counters (what could change under the pen meanwhile), the key
  hashes the evaluated source, so `status` reads stale after such a change even when the landing accepted it.
