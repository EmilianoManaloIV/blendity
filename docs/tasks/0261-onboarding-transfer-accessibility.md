# 0261: onboarding and transfer - the origin picker, the Adobe preset, one brush grammar, a High Contrast theme

- **Status:** done (round 281)
- **Requirements:** R-14
- **Decisions:** ADR 0085 (F7, the transfer round)
- **Model:** main session
- **PR:** #225

## Asked
The HCI pass (ADR 0085), the transfer round: an artist arriving from Unity, Unreal, Photoshop and Substance, or Blender
met Unity's keys and no question; there was no Adobe preset at all (B for the brush, E for the eraser, `[` `]` for the
size); each brush tool had its own size keys or none; the themes were not measured against WCAG's 4.5:1. Transfer of
learning (Singley and Anderson), Nielsen's H2 (match the real world), WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11.

## What changed
- **The origin picker**: the first-run dialog (now shown on every first run, not only on a Low-tier machine) asks
  "Where are you coming from?" - Unity, Unreal, Blender, Photoshop & Substance (Adobe), or "Just start" - and sets
  the keymap preset and the Editor Feel in one go (`apply_origin`); the tier rows follow on a Low machine, a Start
  button closes it otherwise; Edit > Preferences > Keymap keeps the choice changeable, and Help > "Welcome: Where Are
  You Coming From?" asks again.
- **The Adobe preset** (`keymap adobe`): Unity's keys underneath, with B for Paint Mode, V for the Move tool, E for the
  eraser (Paint's erase toggle, `brush.erase`; in any other mode a toast says whose it is), `[` and `]` for the brush
  size, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z as everywhere; Unity's E (the Rotate tool) gives way to the eraser (the toolbar has it).
  Collisions are checked by the keymap test, which runs over every preset.
- **One brush grammar**: `brush.smaller` and `brush.larger` keymap actions (`[` and `]` in every preset) change the
  active brush's size - Sculpt's radius, Paint's size, Weight Paint's radius, Strokes' size, Scatter's radius - by a
  quarter step (x 1.25, / 1.25), a toast saying the new value; the hints of the five tools name the keys; with no
  brush mode on the keys do nothing (a modal's `]` `[` from round 275 take the keys first).
- **A High Contrast theme** (`theme High Contrast`): text against the window, panel, button and field at 4.5:1 or more,
  the accent, warning and error colours at 4.5:1 against the panel, the focus ring 3:1 against its surroundings; a test
  measures every theme's ratios with WCAG's formula and prints them, asserting the High Contrast theme's.
- **`keymap table`**: a console command printing the actions and their chords under every preset as a Markdown table
  (`Editor::keymap_table`), so the manual's chapter 12 can be regenerated by hand; the manual points at it.

## Tests (`tests_round281`)
- The Adobe preset binds B, V, E, `[`, `]` as stated and leaves the Rotate tool unbound; `]` is bound in every preset
  and the eraser in Adobe's only; the keymap test's clash check (round 60) runs over Adobe too.
- `brush.larger` in Sculpt Mode grows the radius by the step and `brush.smaller` shrinks it back; in Paint Mode the
  paint size, and `brush.erase` turns the eraser on; with no brush mode on, both return false.
- The contrast ratios of every theme are printed; the High Contrast theme's text on the window, panel, button and
  field, its dim text, warning, error and accent on the panel are at least 4.5, its focus ring at least 3; the
  function gives 21 for white on black and 1 for a colour on itself.
- The welcome is shown on every first run; `apply_origin("adobe")` sets the Adobe keys on Unity's feel,
  `apply_origin("blender")` Blender's keys and feel; the prefs keep the Adobe keys; `keymap_table` has a row per
  action and a column per preset.

## Not done
- Photoshop's X (swap the foreground and background colours): Paint has one colour, so nothing to swap; Space-drag
  panning: Space is the Wheel's in every preset but SketchUp's (the View tool and the middle button pan).
- The brush step says its value in a toast, not in the status hint (the hint is a table of modes, not of moments).
- Per-origin lines in each layout's guide ("In Unity this was the Layout dropdown").
- The manual's chapter 12 is pasted from `keymap table`, not generated at build time.
- Okabe-Ito polygroup colours and hatching (the GPU view's polygroup shading has no palette table to swap).
- State cues for the eraser (a dashed ring and an "E"); the RECORD cue (round 278) and the Play toolbar tint stand.
- Keyboard reach for every drag-only or right-click-only panel action.
